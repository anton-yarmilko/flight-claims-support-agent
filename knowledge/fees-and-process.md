# Fees and claim process (demo configuration)

The values below are a demo configuration for a fictional service. Replace them with the
company's published price list before use.

## Fees

- No win, no fee: nothing is charged unless compensation is paid.
- Standard success fee: **30%** of the compensation, VAT included.
- If the airline refuses and the case goes to court or an external lawyer: **50%**.
- Baggage and reimbursement claims: **40%**.

## Stages

| Stage | What happens | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submitted | Details and documents received | 1-2 days |
| Sent to airline | Claim filed with the airline | 2-8 weeks for a reply |
| Negotiation | Follow-ups, airline objections | weeks to months |
| Legal / authority | Escalated to a national authority or court | months |
| Paid | Compensation received, fee deducted, payout sent | 5-10 business days |

Never promise a date. Give the current stage and the next step.

## Documents

- Boarding pass or e-ticket (booking confirmation) for every passenger
- ID document for the lead passenger
- Signed authorisation (power of attorney) so the service can act for the customer
